    About the data

    The map covers the full histories indexed under verified PI profiles, including earlier institutions. Indexing, author disambiguation, and missing affiliations can affect coverage; unverified same-name profiles are not included. Publication totals are deduplicated across the two PIs and include indexed works without a mapped collaboration.

    OpenAlex can limit a work’s metadata to its first 100 authorships. indexed works reach this limit and may have incomplete collaboration coverage. Co-located institutions share a marker; their individual publication records remain available in the institution list.
