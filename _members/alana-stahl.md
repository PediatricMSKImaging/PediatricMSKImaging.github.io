Alana Stahl carried out her Master's research in Biomedical Engineering at the McCaig Institute under the supervision of Dr. Danielle Whittier and co-supervision of Dr. Roman Krawetz. Her thesis investigated bone marrow adipose tissue (BMAT), trabecular bone microarchitecture, and the bone marrow lipidome during skeletal growth in mice, with a complementary chapter developing a sex- and age-specific strain-to-force calibration method for [in vivo tibial loading]({{ 'research/projects/bone-mechanoregulation-during-growth' | relative_url }}). Her work combined microCT imaging, lipidomics, and biomechanical testing to better understand how bone and marrow fat change together across the growth period. Outside the lab, Alana can be found in the air doing aerials, coaching others, or outside enjoying the sun!
