Kendall R. Forrester is a Pediatric Medical Radiation Technologist at the Alberta Children’s Hospital. She completed her Bachelor of Health Science from Thompson Rivers University and holds several post graduate certifications in Infection Prevention and Control from Queen’s University as well as a Computed Tomography Imaging Certificate (CTIC) and Certificate in Interventional Radiology (CIR) from the Canadian Association of Medical Radiation Technologists (CAMRT).

Previously, Kendall worked as a research MRT with the Canadian Longitudinal Society of Aging at the University of Calgary. She is also involved with the CAMRT-AB on the Advisory Council, focusing on addressing professional issues for MRT’s in Alberta, as well as, several National committees advocating for education and medical research.

Outside of her work, Kendall is busy with two active kids in dancing, skating and gymnastics. She enjoys CrossFit and cheering on her husband’s various marathons and mountaineering projects.
