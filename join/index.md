<h2 id="current-opportunities"><span class="playful-heading">{% include science-icon.html name="rocket" style="red" %}General Inquiries</span></h2>

We always consider inquiries from prospective graduate and undergraduate students. Explore our <a href="{{ '/research/' | relative_url }}">research</a>, including <a href="{{ '/research/#clinical-projects' | relative_url }}">clinical projects</a> and <a href="{{ '/research/#preclinical-projects' | relative_url }}">preclinical projects</a>, to learn about our work and identify areas that interest you.

We especially encourage applicants from engineering, computer science, biological sciences, biomedical sciences, and biomechanics backgrounds to get in touch.

## What to Include in Your Inquiry

Email Dr. Danielle Whittier at <a href="mailto:danielle.whittier@ucalgary.ca">danielle.whittier@ucalgary.ca</a> with:

- Your CV or resume
- An unofficial transcript
- A short description of your research interests
- Whether you are most interested in computational, imaging, clinical, or experimental work
- Whether you are seeking a graduate or undergraduate opportunity, and your preferred start date

<div>

## What We Value

- Curiosity about pediatric musculoskeletal health
- Interest in collaborative, interdisciplinary research
- Experience or enthusiasm in imaging, data analysis, biology, engineering, or biomechanics
