Available Studies

The studies below are actively recruiting. View each study’s recruitment page for eligibility details and to express interest in participating.

Body Composition and Bone Growth Study Recruiting Participation includes bone imaging scans and questionnaires. Bone Marrow Adipose Tissue in Paediatric Populations Recruiting Participation involves MRI scans to measure bone marrow fat.

What Participation Involves

Activities and time commitments vary by study. Please email pediatricmsk@ucalgary.ca or call 403-210-7368 to confirm eligibility, visit length, and any preparation with our study coordinator.

Planning Your Visit

Our lab is in the Health Research Innovation Centre at the Foothills Medical Centre campus. Some study visits take place at Alberta Children’s Hospital; follow the location details in your appointment confirmation. See our contact details and map to the lab.

Patient Partnership

Patient partners help shape our research by sharing lived experience, priorities, and feedback on how we communicate study goals and results. To explore patient or community partnership, please contact us. Learn more through the CIHR Patient Engagement resources.
