Grace Mutasingwa

Past member · Undergraduate Student

Grace completed her BSc in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Calgary, where she performed her final year research project in the lab involving micro-finite element bone modeling for the study of bone mechanical behavior using microCT scans of mouse tibia bones. She is currently pursuing a Master of Engineering in Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering with an interest in biomechanics and computational modeling.
